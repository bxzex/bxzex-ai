# Short video scripts

People decide in the first two seconds. Write those first.

**Structure for 15 to 60 seconds**
1. **Hook (0 to 2s):** the result, the claim or the question, said out loud and shown on screen. "I rebuilt this site in one day. Here's the before."
2. **Why it matters (2 to 6s):** one sentence on what they get by watching.
3. **The thing (the middle):** three beats at most. Show, do not describe. One idea per shot.
4. **Payoff:** the reveal, the number, the after.
5. **One ask:** follow, comment a word, link in bio. One.

**Writing it**
- Write for the ear. Short sentences. Words you would say.
- About 2.5 words per second. A 30 second video is around 75 words.
- Every line should make them want the next one. Cut any line that does not.
- No "Hey guys, welcome back". Start in the middle of the action.
- Put the hook as on-screen text too. Most people watch muted.

**Format the script as**
- `VISUAL:` what is on screen
- `SAY:` the spoken line
- `TEXT:` on-screen caption, if any

Give three alternative hooks at the top. Add a caption and three to five specific hashtags at the end.
