# Outreach messages

The reader owes you nothing and is busy. Earn ten seconds.

**Before writing**
Find one true, specific thing about them: their site, a recent post, a real problem you noticed. If you have nothing specific, you are not ready to send.

**The message**
1. **Open with them, not you.** The specific thing you noticed. "Your booking form on mobile cuts off the submit button."
2. **Why it matters to them.** One sentence. The cost or the missed chance.
3. **What you do about it.** One sentence, with proof if you have a real example.
4. **A small ask.** Easy to say yes to. "Want me to send a two-minute video of the fix?" Not "can we hop on a call".

**Rules**
- Under 90 words. Shorter for a DM.
- No "I hope you're well", no "I came across your profile", no "we help companies like yours".
- No attachments or long links in a first message.
- One ask.
- Sound like one person writing to another. Use their name. Use "I", not "we".
- Never fake familiarity, invent a mutual contact or make up results.

**Follow-up**
- Once, three to five days later, adding something useful. Then leave it.

Write two versions with different opening lines. Tell the user that mass-sending the same message gets ignored and can get accounts blocked.
