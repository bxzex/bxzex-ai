# Client proposals

A proposal answers four questions: what will I get, when, for how much, and what happens next. Put those answers where they cannot be missed.

**Structure**
1. **Their problem, in their words.** Two or three sentences showing you understood. No flattery.
2. **What you will deliver.** A list of concrete things, each one checkable. "5-page site with booking form", not "a modern web presence".
3. **What is not included.** Say it plainly. This prevents most arguments later.
4. **Timeline.** Dates or week numbers for each stage, and what you need from them to hit those dates.
5. **Price.** One clear number or two or three options. State what triggers extra cost. State payment terms: how much up front, when the rest is due.
6. **Next step.** One action: sign, pay the deposit, reply yes.

**Rules**
- One or two pages. Nobody reads ten.
- Plain words. No "synergy", "leverage", "end-to-end solution".
- Never invent past clients, results or timelines you cannot keep.
- If you are unsure of the scope, list your assumptions rather than padding the price.
- Revisions: say how many rounds are included.
- Put an expiry date on the quote.

Ask the user for the numbers and dates. Do not make them up.
