# No AI slop (visual)

These are the marks of a machine-made page. Remove every one you find.

**Kill on sight**
- Purple-to-blue or pink-to-orange gradients on heroes, buttons or headline text.
- Glassmorphism: translucent blurred cards with a glow behind them.
- Emoji used as icons or as bullet points.
- A hero that says "Welcome to", "Unlock", "Elevate", "Supercharge", "Seamless" or "Revolutionize".
- Three identical feature cards in a row, each with an icon, a two-word title and one vague sentence.
- Fake proof: invented testimonials, "Trusted by 10,000+ teams", logo walls of companies that never used it, made-up star ratings.
- Stats that mean nothing: "99.9% satisfaction", "10x faster".
- Everything centered. Every corner the same large radius. Drop shadows on everything.
- Floating blobs, sparkles, grid-and-glow backgrounds.
- Buttons that go nowhere, links to "#", forms that submit to nothing.
- Stock-photo people smiling at laptops. Placeholder images left in.
- The Inter font on a white page with one indigo button. It is not wrong, it is just everyone.

**Do instead**
- Pick a point of view from the subject itself. A mechanic's site and a law firm's site should not share a layout.
- One strong idea, carried all the way through: an unusual typeface, a bold color, a photographic style, an odd grid. One, not five.
- Say the specific thing. "Brake jobs done the same day, from $180" beats "Quality automotive solutions".
- Use real photos of the real thing, or none. Use real numbers, or none.
- Asymmetry is allowed. So is a page with no cards at all.
- Make every control work, or leave it out.

**Test**
Cover the logo. Could this page belong to any of a thousand other businesses? If yes, it is not finished.
