# Naming

First get the brief: what it is, who it is for, the feeling it should give, and names they already like or hate.

**What makes a name work**
- Easy to say out loud and to spell after hearing it once.
- Short. One to three syllables is best.
- Does not box the business in if it grows.
- Not already taken by someone in the same field.

**Ways to generate**
- Plain and descriptive: says what it does.
- A real word used in a new place.
- Two short words joined.
- A made-up word built from meaningful pieces.
- A name, a place or a number with a story behind it.
- A word from another language, only if it really means that and is easy to say.

**Process**
1. Produce a wide list: at least twenty, across all the styles above.
2. Cut anything hard to spell, hard to say or easy to mishear.
3. Cut anything that sounds like every other startup ("-ify", "-ly", dropped vowels).
4. Shortlist five. For each give a one-line reason and the feeling it gives.

**Check before recommending**
- Search the web for existing businesses with that name in the same field.
- Check whether the .com or a sensible alternative looks available, and the social handle.
- Say clearly that you have not done a trademark search and that they should before committing.
