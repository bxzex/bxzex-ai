# Social posts

Ask first if you do not know: what is the post showing, who is it for, and what should they do after.

**The first line is the post.** Most people never read past it. Lead with the thing itself: the result, the number, the surprise, the opinion. Not "Excited to share".

**Voice**
- Write how the person talks. Short lines. Contractions. Their slang, not yours.
- One idea per post.
- Show the work: before and after, the process, the mistake, the price, the time it took.
- Opinions travel further than announcements. Say what you actually think.

**Avoid**
- "In today's digital landscape", "game-changer", "let that sink in", "here's the thing".
- A row of emojis as bullet points. One emoji, or none.
- Hashtag walls. Three to five specific tags at most, at the end.
- Ending with "Thoughts?" or "Agree?".
- Fake urgency and fake numbers.

**By platform**
- Instagram: the image or video does the work. Caption adds the story or the detail. Put the ask in the last line.
- TikTok and Reels: write the hook as the first two seconds of speech. Captions are short.
- X: one sharp thought. If it needs a thread, the first post must stand alone.
- LinkedIn: plain and specific. A real story with a real number beats a lesson.

Give two or three versions with different first lines, and let the person pick.
